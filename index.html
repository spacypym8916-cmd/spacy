What PlaylistCut does

Playlists grow for years and nobody cleans them up. PlaylistCut handles the tedious edits in one pass, and you review every change before it is saved.

DEDUPE

Find duplicates

Catch the same song added twice, including live, remastered and radio-edit versions that are easy to miss.

CUT TO LENGTH

Fit a time limit

Set a target such as 45 or 90 minutes, and PlaylistCut proposes which tracks to keep.

MERGE / SPLIT

Merge and split lists

Combine several playlists into one clean list, or break a huge one into smaller lists by mood, era or artist.

REORDER

Reorder with a reason

Sort by artist, year or length, or ask in plain words, like “start slow and build up”.
